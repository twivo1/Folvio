Document editing upgrade: a working Crop, a new text editor, screenshots and images in Auto Change, and
Recognize Text with experimental handwriting recognition.

## Combine · Crop

- **Apply crop now shows the result**: the area outside the frame disappears, the page gets its new size, the
  view switches to Select and fits the page, the thumbnail is updated and *Crop applied* is shown.
- Crop stays non-destructive: Undo/Redo, choosing Crop again (the whole page with the current frame), Reset,
  `.folvio` projects and PDF export all use the same crop rectangle.
- **Annotations stay on their content** when cropping, resetting or rotating (they used to keep their position
  relative to the page frame and slid off the words). Zoom never changes annotations.
- The coordinate systems (source → straightened → cropped → page units → screen) are documented in
  `docs/COMBINE.md`.

## Text editor

- **Transparent text boxes**: no white rectangle while typing; only a thin dashed frame, a *Move* tab and resize
  handles, none of which is exported.
- **Formatting applies immediately** to the text being typed: font, size, colour (also through the colour
  dialog, which no longer ends the edit), opacity, bold/italic/underline, alignment.
- **Move and resize while typing or selected**: width changes re-wrap the text without scaling the font;
  *Auto size* keeps the box as tall as the text, a taller box can be set but never clips the text.
- Text tool: click to add, click a text to edit, drag a text to move it. Select tool: drag moves, a second
  click edits. Esc or Ctrl+Enter finishes (Esc used to throw the text away). Ctrl+Y redoes inside the box on
  Linux too. **Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V** copy and paste annotations (a text box also puts its text on the clipboard).

## Auto Change · screenshots and images

- **Ctrl+V on the Auto Change start page** opens a screenshot (Win+Shift+S, Print Screen, a copied image or an
  image file copied in Explorer/Files, X11/Wayland `image/png`) in the new **image editor**: Annotate (all
  Combine tools), **Recognize Text**, **Convert to PDF**, **Export Image** (PNG/JPG/WebP/BMP). Images can also be
  dropped or opened there. The original file is never changed.
- **Ctrl+V in an open PDF** inserts an image as a real image object: select, move, resize (corners keep the
  proportions; Shift for free), **crop** (non-destructive until saved), copy, **delete**; every step is
  undoable. Text on the clipboard becomes a new text box. Saving still verifies the result - and now also
  checks that all original images are still on the page plus exactly the inserted ones.
- One rule for what a paste means (files first, then a bitmap, then text; a Word/LibreOffice "picture of the
  copied text" is treated as text).

## Recognize Text (printed and handwriting)

- Drag a rectangle; the area is read at full resolution. *Auto*, *Printed text* (Tesseract), *Handwriting
  (experimental)*. The **Recognized Text** panel is editable and shows the confidence and warnings (low
  confidence, formulas/drawings).
- **Copy**, **Insert as Text**, **Replace Selection…** with a **Before / After** preview and four variants:
  overlay, keep and add below, replace on the matched paper colour, clean note. Uneven backgrounds (grid,
  lines) are detected and the overlay is suggested. The photo is never modified; one Undo restores the area.
- **Handwriting** uses PaddleOCR PP-OCRv6 small (Apache-2.0) through ONNX Runtime (MIT), fully offline.
  The models (31 MB) are downloaded **only after you agree**, from PaddlePaddle's repositories at fixed
  versions, verified with SHA-256, and can be removed at any time. Nothing is uploaded, no account or key.
  Quality is **experimental**: on photos of real English handwriting (GNHK) it finds 57 % of the words exactly
  (Tesseract: 7 %); Polish handwriting has not yet been measured on real samples. Details:
  `docs/HANDWRITING_OCR.md`.
